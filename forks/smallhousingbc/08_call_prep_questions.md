# Call Prep — Question Bank for the Disco

A discovery call's job is to learn enough to come back with a concrete proposal. This is the question bank, organized by category, with the most important questions at the top of each section. Pick 8-12 to actually ask; the rest are backup.

---

## Open the call with

> *"Before we get into BuildOS specifically — can you walk me through what made you reach out, and what 'a custom fork of BuildOS' means in your head right now?"*

This is the single most important question. Their answer tells you which of the three product wedges they're already imagining, what scope they have in mind, and whether they're thinking white-label, configuration, or true fork. Everything downstream calibrates off this.

---

## Section A — Where they are right now (the situation)

1. Who at SHBC has been pushing the conversation about a software platform internally? (Identifies the champion.)
2. What triggered now specifically? Is this tied to Bill 25's June 2026 deadline, a funder conversation, a board ask, or something else?
3. How are you measuring success at SHBC today — number of homeowners who actually completed a project, number of builders trained, policy wins, or something else?
4. Where in the homeowner-developer journey do you see the most drop-off today? When do people quit?
5. What did you build the Toolbox for, and what has actually happened with it? Are you happy with the usage and outcomes?

---

## Section B — What they want from a "fork" (the desired state)

6. If we shipped one thing in 6 months that made a real difference for SHBC's mission, what would it be?
7. Who's the primary user you're picturing — the homeowner, the builder, the planner, or all three?
8. Is the goal a tool *you operate*, a tool *your members use*, or a tool *the public uses*?
9. White-label, configured BuildOS, or fully separate fork — do you have a preference, and why?
10. What's your stance on open-source? Are you OK with the platform being open and forkable by anyone, or do you want some surface-level proprietary IP for SHBC?

---

## Section C — Workflow and product specifics

11. Walk me through the journey of a typical homeowner who reaches out to SHBC today. What do you do for them, and where do they hit walls?
12. The Multiplex Construction Training cohorts — what tools do those builders use today on real projects? Spreadsheets, Buildertrend, nothing?
13. Have you ever asked your builder/homeowner network what software they wish existed?
14. How are you currently feeding Toolbox content to homeowners — search, curated lists, workshops?
15. Do you have any kind of CRM or contact database tying together homeowners, builders, municipalities, funders?

---

## Section D — Funding, commercials, ownership

16. How do you see this getting funded — your operating budget, a CMHC bid, a municipal procurement, a foundation grant, something else?
17. Have you talked to CMHC, REFBC, or Vancity about this specifically yet?
18. Is there a funder relationship you'd want to pull in early — someone you'd want us to co-pitch with?
19. What's your bandwidth on the SHBC side? Who would actually own the relationship from your end?
20. If we co-developed a tool, how would you want IP to work — joint, FutureBuild-owned, SHBC-owned, public domain?
21. What does success look like commercially for SHBC over a 3-year horizon? Revenue, fundraising, member growth, brand?

---

## Section E — Competitive / ecosystem

22. How do you think about DASH (BC Housing's accelerated housing platform)? Adjacent, competitive, or complementary?
23. Are there other tech vendors you've talked to about building this? What did or didn't work?
24. Are any of your municipal partners (Vancouver, Maple Ridge, Nelson, others) asking for a tool right now?
25. Where do you think the Province (Ministry of Housing) sits on this kind of platform? Would they fund or buy it?

---

## Section F — Governance and risk

26. The Smallworks–SHBC overlap (Jake, Akua) — how do you handle perceived conflict-of-interest if a builder-marketplace feature launches?
27. Who at SHBC has decision rights on a partnership of this scope? Is this a Daniel-decides, Tamara-decides, or board-decides call?
28. What's your timeline pressure? Anything tied to the June 2026 bylaw deadline, an event, or a fiscal year?

---

## Section G — Questions that earn trust (use sparingly, when right)

29. *"What's the dumbest thing other people in BC are doing about gentle density that drives you crazy?"* (Earns a real answer.)
30. *"If this conversation goes nowhere, what's your plan B for getting a platform built?"* (Tells you the urgency and your real competition.)
31. *"What would make you regret picking us as the partner?"* (Surfaces real concerns up front.)

---

## What to absolutely *avoid* on the call

- Avoid pitching all three product wedges. Pick the one that matches their answer to the opening question and go deep on that.
- Avoid name-dropping individual SHBC team members who aren't on the call (especially Jake Fry — Smallworks framing is touchy).
- Avoid claiming the Toolbox is broken or insufficient. It's their product. Frame BuildOS as "what comes next after the Toolbox" not "a replacement for it."
- Avoid promising delivery timelines that beat the June 2026 bylaw deadline.
- Avoid pricing discussions in the disco call. Get to "we're aligned on the problem and the wedge" first; pricing follows in a separate session with scope.

---

## After the call — your prep

Send them a recap email within 24 hours that:
1. Reflects back the wedge they pointed to as the highest priority
2. Names the funding path you'll explore together
3. Proposes a concrete next step (whiteboard session, joint discovery doc, intro to a CMHC contact, or a 2-week scoping engagement)
4. Lists 2-3 things you'll do on your side before the next conversation

Keep the email under 200 words.
